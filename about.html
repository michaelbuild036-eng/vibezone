About VibeZone

Welcome to VibeZone — a place for games, entertainment, challenges, friendly competition and connecting with people.

VibeZone brings together fun online activities and community features in one place, designed to give users a place to play, explore and enjoy themselves.

What you can do on VibeZone

  • Play games: enjoy casual games and interactive activities.
  • Would You Rather: choose between fun, difficult or interesting scenarios.
  • Guess the Emoji: test your ability to solve emoji puzzles.
  • Trivia Challenge: challenge your knowledge across different topics.
  • Truth or Dare: enjoy a classic interactive party game.
  • Personality Quiz: explore fun personality-style quizzes.
  • Challenges: take part in activities and track your progress.
  • Points and Leaderboards: earn points through supported activities and compare scores.
  • Profiles: personalise your presence using the available profile options.
  • Anonymous inbox: receive messages through your VibeZone inbox link.

VibeAI Corner

VibeZone may include VibeAI Corner, featuring interactive fictional characters and demonstration conversations. Demo characters and preset responses are entertainment features and are not necessarily live AI services.

Our goal

Our goal is to create an enjoyable online space where people can play games, discover challenges, compete for fun and interact with others.

Keep the vibes positive.

Respect others, play fairly, avoid abusive behaviour and help make VibeZone a welcoming community.

Safety and community

VibeZone provides reporting features for users to report messages or behaviour that may violate platform rules. Users should not use messaging features to bully, harass, threaten, impersonate or harm others.

Blocking currently works within the browser where it is used and does not guarantee that messages are prevented across the platform or on other devices.

Who can use VibeZone?

VibeZone is strictly for adults aged 18 and older.

Anyone under the age of 18 is not permitted to register or use VibeZone.
