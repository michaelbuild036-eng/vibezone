Privacy Policy

Learn what information VibeZone may process and how it is used.

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VibeZone handles information when you use the VibeZone website, games, and community features. VibeZone is operated by Enhle Thalente Chamane, an individual based in South Africa.

18+ only: VibeZone is intended for people aged 18 or older. Anyone under 18 must not register or use the service.

1. Information that may be collected

Depending on the features you use, VibeZone may process:

  • Account information: an authentication identifier created through Firebase Authentication and information associated with your account.
  • Profile information: your nickname, avatar, and any optional bio or profile photo you provide.
  • Activity information: game results, scores, points, daily challenge activity, and leaderboard-related information.
  • Messages and reports: content you submit through anonymous inbox messaging and reports you choose to send.
  • Technical information: information processed by the website host or Firebase to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in your nickname, bio, messages, or other content.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to provide account and profile features, operate games and challenges, display leaderboard results, deliver inbox messages, review user reports, maintain site functionality, and help address misuse.

3. Information visible to other users

Some profile details, scores, points, and leaderboard information may be visible to signed-in VibeZone users. Messages are delivered to the intended inbox. Do not assume that content is private if the feature displays or shares it with other users. Avoid sharing details that identify you or someone else.

4. Service providers

VibeZone uses third-party services, including Firebase for authentication and database functionality and GitHub Pages for website hosting. Those providers may process information as needed to provide their services, under their own terms and privacy documentation. VibeZone does not control their independent practices.

5. Retention and security

Information may be retained as long as needed to operate VibeZone, maintain account records, respond to reports, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Reasonable steps are taken to protect information, but no website, database, or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and requests

To ask a question about your information or request assistance with access, correction, or deletion, contact thalenteenhle17@gmail.com. We may need to verify that a request relates to your account. Some records may need to be retained where required or permitted by law.

7. South African privacy law

VibeZone aims to handle personal information responsibly and with regard to applicable South African privacy requirements, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), where applicable. Your rights and available remedies depend on the law that applies to your circumstances.

8. Children

VibeZone is for adults aged 18 and older and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly invite anyone under 18 to register. We do not currently claim to use a formal age-verification system. If you believe someone under 18 has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the matter.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as VibeZone changes. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact
