Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VibeZone handles information when you use our website, games, profiles, messaging features and other services.

Age restriction: VibeZone is strictly intended for individuals aged 18 and older. Anyone under 18 is prohibited from using the platform.

1. Who operates VibeZone?

VibeZone is independently operated by Enhle Thalente Chamane, an individual based in South Africa. VibeZone is an independently operated project and is not represented as a registered company.

Privacy enquiries can be directed to: thalenteenhle17@gmail.com

2. Information we may collect or process

Depending on the features you use, VibeZone may process:

  • Nicknames and profile information.
  • Profile pictures and avatars you choose to upload or select.
  • Game scores, points, challenges and leaderboard information.
  • Messages submitted through anonymous inboxes.
  • Technical identifiers used for authentication, message delivery and security.
  • Message reports, report reasons and information related to reported content.
  • Browser and technical information needed to operate and troubleshoot the website.

3. Browser storage

Some VibeZone features may store information locally in your browser, including profile settings, preferences, game-related information and blocked sender settings.

This information may remain on your device until you clear your browser's site data, remove it, or the application changes it. Clearing browser data may remove locally stored information.

4. Firebase and online storage

VibeZone uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to support online features such as anonymous authentication, inbox messaging, reports and leaderboard information.

Information submitted through these features may be stored and processed by Firebase to provide the functionality of VibeZone.

5. Profiles and public information

Information you choose to display on your profile may be visible to other users. This may include your nickname, avatar, profile picture, biography and points or leaderboard information.

Do not publish passwords, private contact details, financial information or other sensitive personal information in public profile fields.

6. Anonymous messaging

VibeZone offers anonymous messaging features. Anonymous messaging means that the sender's identity is not displayed to the recipient in the ordinary message interface.

Anonymous messaging does not guarantee absolute anonymity. Technical identifiers and information may be processed by VibeZone's systems and service providers for message delivery, security, troubleshooting and abuse reporting.

Anonymous messaging must not be used for harassment, threats, bullying, impersonation, exploitation or other abusive behaviour.

7. Reports and blocking

Users may report messages through the available reporting controls. A report may include message content, identifiers, the selected report reason and information needed to review the complaint.

The current blocking feature operates within the browser where the block is applied. It hides messages from the blocked sender in that browser but does not necessarily prevent messages from being sent to the server or guarantee blocking across other devices.

8. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • Operate profiles, games, quizzes, challenges and leaderboards.
  • Provide inbox and messaging functionality.
  • Store and display points and game results.
  • Review reports and address abuse.
  • Maintain website functionality and security.
  • Diagnose errors and technical issues.
  • Respond to enquiries and privacy requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

9. Notifications

If browser notifications are offered and you choose to enable them, your browser may display notifications according to the permissions you grant. You can change notification permissions through your browser settings.

10. Third-party services

VibeZone relies on third-party services, including GitHub Pages and Google Firebase, to host and operate parts of the platform.

These providers may process information in accordance with their own service terms, privacy policies and technical requirements.

VibeZone does not sell users' personal information.

11. Retention and deletion

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the relevant features, maintain security, address reports, respond to requests or meet applicable legal obligations.

Information stored locally can generally be removed by clearing VibeZone's website data in your browser.

For requests concerning access to, correction of or deletion of information associated with your use of VibeZone, email thalenteenhle17@gmail.com.

Requests may require reasonable verification to protect information from unauthorised disclosure. Some information may be retained where permitted or required by law.

12. Your privacy rights

Applicable privacy laws may give you rights concerning your personal information, including rights to request access, correction or deletion, subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

South African users may have rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). You may contact VibeZone using the contact information below or contact the South African Information Regulator where applicable.

13. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect the platform and information it handles. However, no website, electronic storage system or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

14. International processing

Third-party providers may process information in locations outside South Africa. Such processing may be subject to the providers' service arrangements and applicable legal requirements.

15. Policy changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as VibeZone develops or changes its features. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
