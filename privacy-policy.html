# VibeZone Privacy Policy **Last updated: 17 November 2026** ## 1. Who operates VibeZone VibeZone is independently owned and operated by **Enhle Thalente Chamane**, an individual based in South Africa. VibeZone is not currently operated through a registered business. **Support Email:** thalenteenhle17@gmail.com ## 2. Information We Process Depending on the features you use, VibeZone may process: - Randomly generated Firebase account identifiers. - Nicknames and avatars. - Optional profile pictures and biographies. - Game scores, points and daily challenge progress. - Anonymous messages. - Reports about inappropriate messages. - Technical information necessary to operate and protect the website. ## 3. Public Profile Information Your nickname, avatar, optional biography, profile picture and leaderboard score may be visible to other VibeZone visitors. Do not include your home address, phone number, passwords, banking details, school or other private information in public profile fields. ## 4. Anonymous Messaging VibeZone allows users to send messages displayed as anonymous to recipients. Anonymous messaging does not guarantee absolute anonymity. Technical identifiers may be processed to operate the service, prevent abuse and investigate reports. Users must not use anonymous messaging to bully, threaten, harass or harm others. ## 5. Service Providers and Storage VibeZone currently uses: - Google Firebase for authentication and database services. - GitHub Pages for website hosting. These providers may process information in countries where their infrastructure operates. Their respective privacy policies and terms also apply. ## 6. Notifications If you grant browser notification permission, VibeZone may display notifications about new inbox messages. You can revoke notification permission through your browser or device settings. Background push notifications require additional Firebase Cloud Messaging configuration. ## 7. Data Retention and Deletion VibeZone retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain accounts, provide features, prevent abuse and meet applicable legal obligations. To request account or personal information deletion, contact: **thalenteenhle17@gmail.com** Deletion requests will be reviewed manually by the VibeZone owner. Certain records may need to be retained for security or legal reasons. ## 8. Age Requirements and Younger Users VibeZone is intended for individuals aged **16 and older**. Individuals under 16 must not create accounts or use the platform. Because VibeZone includes public profiles, leaderboards and anonymous messaging, users aged 16 and 17 should take additional care when sharing personal information. We encourage younger users to: - Never share their home address or phone number. - Never share passwords or banking details. - Report bullying, harassment or threatening messages. - Avoid sharing private photographs or sensitive information. - Speak to a trusted adult if they experience online harassment or feel unsafe. VibeZone will take reasonable steps to protect personal information in accordance with applicable South African privacy laws, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). ## 9. Your Privacy Rights Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information. For privacy requests, contact: **thalenteenhle17@gmail.com** ## 10. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when VibeZone introduces new features, changes service providers or modifies its data practices.
