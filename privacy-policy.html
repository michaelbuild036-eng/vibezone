VibeZone is not currently operated through a registered business. For privacy questions, support requests or complaints, contact: **Email:** thalenteenhle17@gmail.com ## 2. Information we process Depending on the features you use, VibeZone may process: - Your randomly generated Firebase account identifier. - Nickname and avatar. - Optional profile picture and biography. - Game scores, points and daily challenge progress. - Anonymous messages sent to inboxes. - Reports submitted about inappropriate messages. - Technical information necessary to operate and protect the website. ## 3. Public profile information Your nickname, avatar, optional biography, profile picture and leaderboard score may be visible to other VibeZone visitors. Do not include your home address, phone number, passwords, banking details, school, workplace or other private information in public profile fields. ## 4. Anonymous messaging VibeZone allows users to send messages that are displayed as anonymous to the recipient. Anonymous messaging does not guarantee absolute anonymity. Technical identifiers may be processed to operate the service, prevent abuse and investigate reports. Users should not use anonymous messaging to harass, threaten, bully or harm others. ## 5. Service providers and storage VibeZone currently uses: - **Google Firebase:** Authentication and database services. - **GitHub Pages:** Website hosting. These providers may process information in countries where their infrastructure operates. Their respective privacy policies and terms also apply to their services. ## 6. Notifications If you grant browser notification permission, VibeZone may display notifications about new inbox messages. You can revoke notification permission through your browser or device settings. Background push notifications require additional Firebase Cloud Messaging setup and are not guaranteed to be available. ## 7. Data retention and deletion VibeZone retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain accounts, provide features, prevent abuse and meet applicable legal obligations. If you want your account or personal information deleted, contact: **thalenteenhle17@gmail.com** Deletion requests will be reviewed and processed manually by the VibeZone owner. Certain records may need to be retained where required for security, legal or legitimate operational reasons. ## 8. Children and safety VibeZone is intended for individuals aged **18 and older**. The service is not intended for children under 18. Individuals below this age should not create accounts or submit personal information through VibeZone. Users must not share threats, harassment, sexual exploitation material, hate speech, private personal information or illegal content. ## 9.
